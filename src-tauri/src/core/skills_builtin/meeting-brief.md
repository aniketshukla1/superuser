Produce `meeting-brief.md`, structured exactly like this:

# <Meeting> — brief
**Who:** attendees and their roles
**Goal:** the one outcome that makes this meeting worth holding

## Context
What has already happened. Pull from memory, calendar and mail if you have them;
cite where each fact came from.

## Agenda
Three to five items, each with a time box.

## Talking points
What the user should say, in their voice — bullets, not paragraphs.

## Likely questions
Questions the other side will ask, each with a one-line answer.

## Open items
What you could not determine and the user should confirm before the meeting.

Ground every claim in something you actually read. If you could not find context,
say so plainly in Open items rather than filling the brief with plausible guesses.
